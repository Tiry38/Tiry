# WoW Forever economy as of 2026-11-19: prices, crafting meta, AH conditions, gold at level ~36 (focus: Cooking and Leatherworking)

Source caveats for the report writer:
- **Almost all live price data comes from timesaver.gg** (it sells gold and boosts, so treat it as **[commercial]**). Its numbers are medians from its own AH scans. Its calculators default to **US Alliance**. Requests with `?region=eu&faction=horde` still returned US Alliance, so **I found no EU Horde-specific prices**. The only EU numbers are on the timesaver auction-prices overview (all EU realms, both factions) and in the Nov 11 LW article.
- I found **no reachable Reddit, MMO-Champion or YouTube primary data** on Forever's economy. They did not surface in search, matching our own Nov 8 and Nov 10 experience. German sites (mein-mmo, buffed, pcgames) cover Forever only in general terms (black market, gold buying). They have no price data.
- **Classic Era proxy data was not used.**
- Prior local research: `wow-addons/docs/GOLD-RECHERCHE-2026-11.md` (Nov 10) and `RECHERCHE-Anhang.md` (Nov 8). Below, "update vs. Nov 10" means a change from those notes.

## 1. Which gold-making methods are reported as profitable in Forever (numbers, dates)?

### Takeaway
Reported profitable methods are **gathering and selling raw materials** (Skinning, Fishing), **intermediates with thin supply** (Cured Rugged Hide, Salt Shaker cooldown), **Thick armor kits** (small margin, crowded) and **a few niche Cooking crafts** (Spinefin Halibut, Goldthorn Tea). Most leveling food and most new LW gear lose money or can't be sold. **For a level ~36 character, the guides say leveling on is usually faster to the 40 mount than farming.**

### Cited Findings
- **Cured Rugged Hide (LW 225, ×2 with your own salt):** margin per craft after the 5% AH cut is **US 75g / EU 43g**. One Cured Rugged Hide sells for US ~39g 98s–40g and **EU 22g 77s**. Rugged Hide costs 89s in the US (only 30 listed across 3 US markets). Refined Deeprock Salt costs ~52g (5 listed); Deeprock Salt is under 3s; a Salt Shaker costs 14g US / 13g EU. Prices verified Nov 11, 09:15 UTC; article updated Nov 16 — [timesaver LW recipes, Nov 11/16 [commercial]](https://timesaver.gg/blog/wow-forever-leatherworking-recipes-gold)
- The same article calls the **Salt Shaker cooldown output** the best margin it found. It says Thick armor kits sell but have **over 150 of each kit listed**, so a price drop would erase the margin. **Commerce Authority gear sets are Bind on Pickup** and can't be sold — [timesaver LW, Nov 11 [commercial]](https://timesaver.gg/blog/wow-forever-leatherworking-recipes-gold)
- **Forceful Thick Armor Kit (LW 185):** materials 50s US / 48s EU, listing median 85s / 70s, margin **30s / 18s**. **Mystic Thick (185):** materials 54s / 48s, median 89s / 77s, margin **30s / 25s**. **Forceful Rugged (235):** loses money in the EU (materials 19g 08s vs. median 15g 01s). Mystic Rugged has no listings. Medium and Heavy kits list at 11–29s in the US and are "mostly for skill-ups". Light Leather Reagent Bag margin: 2s US / 1s EU — [timesaver LW, Nov 11 [commercial]](https://timesaver.gg/blog/wow-forever-leatherworking-recipes-gold)
- **LW crafts profitable on Nov 19 (US Alliance, after 5% cut):** Dusky Leather Armor costs 45s 89c and sells for 94s 5c (**+48s 16c**). Dragonscale Gauntlets cost 3g 50s and sell for 3g 89s (+38s 70c). Fine Leather Belt: +1s 53c. **Camp Tent** costs 2s 50c and sells for 4s 27c (+1s 77c) — [timesaver LW calculator, scan Nov 19 20:25 UTC [commercial]](https://timesaver.gg/tools/wow-forever/professions/leatherworking?region=eu&faction=horde)
- **Cooking crafts profitable on Nov 19 (US Alliance):** **Spinefin Halibut (210)** costs 1s 77c and sells for 61s 75c (+59s 98c, **only 35 listed**). **Goldthorn Tea (175, makes 4)** costs 6s 62c and sells for 15s 16c (+8s 54c, ~2,045 listed). Basic Campfire Kit: +61c. **Losing money:** Roasted Boar Meat, Crab Cake, Goblin Deviled Clams, Boiled Clams, Herb Baked Egg, Spiced/Charred Wolf Meat, Dry Pork Ribs, Spider Sausage (−1s), Coyote Steak (−4s 5c) — [timesaver Cooking calculator, scan Nov 19 20:25 UTC [commercial]](https://timesaver.gg/tools/wow-forever/professions/cooking)
- The same page says Spinefin Halibut **sells to a vendor for 2s**. Against the 61s 75c AH value, that points to a thin or outlier market (the page itself doesn't explain the gap) — [timesaver Cooking](https://timesaver.gg/tools/wow-forever/professions/cooking)
- **Conquestcapped (updated Nov 16) on levels 30–40:** sell materials and crafts for levels 20–35 to the players behind you. Craft what quests ask for (Nov 13: Moonsteel Broadsword sold for about 1g 88s against about 1g 55s in materials). Pick quest rewards by vendor value (a level 30 two-hander at 72s vs. gloves at 13s). Blue Pearl: ~40s (Nov 9) → ~15s (Nov 13). **Keeping on leveling is usually faster to the mount than farming at 38.** Discount gold-per-hour figures by ~30% — [conquestcapped gold guide, Nov 16 [commercial]](https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-gold-making/)
- Beta or early gold-per-hour figures (not Nov data): Skinning at levels 17–20 makes ~2g/h, up to ~7.5g/h with Pristine drops. Fishing at 225 makes ">10g/h for some". Mining at levels 22–24 makes ~7g/h. The SM chest loop (~40g/h) was hotfixed around Oct 19 — [conquestcapped](https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-gold-making/)
- Profession tier list: Skinning is top (Pristine Leather is the most used Scarce material). Fishing and Cooking are top-tier (food with +5% XP and fish oils sell all the time). Leatherworking is **B-tier** — [boosting.pro [commercial]](https://boosting.pro/wow-forever-gold-guide/)
- Cooking: **crafted food gives "Well Fed" +5% XP from kills**, so there is leveling demand from day one — [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/news/crafted-food-in-wow-forever-gives-you-5-more-xp-from-kills/); [Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/cooked-food-provides-an-xp-buff-in-world-of-warcraft-forever). Cooked fish sold for ~1s each at busy quest hubs in the beta — [conquestcapped](https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-gold-making/)

### Inferences
- At LW skill ~185–225 (realistic at level ~36), the best reported margins are **Cured Rugged Hide** (if Rugged Hide is available; it is a lvl 45+ hide) and **Dusky Leather Armor** (~48s, US data only). Thick kits make ~18–30s each but are crowded. Medium/Heavy kits are for skill-ups only.
- For Cooking, the big gains are in niche or thin crafts. Classic leveling food (wolf, boar, clams) **sells below material cost**. The supply is huge (hundreds to thousands of listings per craft).
- EU vs. US: EU margins on LW intermediates are lower (Cured Rugged Hide 43g vs. 75g).

### Gaps
- No EU Horde-specific price list (the calculator ignored the URL parameters). No German realm data.
- No sales rate or sell-through data, only listing medians and listing counts.
- No Reddit/MMO-Champion/YouTube reports with Forever numbers were found or reachable.

## 3. Forever-specific changes affecting the economy (hotfixes, AH, vendors, mount, gold sellers, population)

### Takeaway
**The 1-copper minimum deposit** was announced on Nov 10, **pulled on Nov 12**, and rescheduled for the weekly maintenance on **Nov 17 (US) / Nov 18 (EU)**. As of Nov 19 it is confirmed **only by player reports**; Blizzard hasn't confirmed it, so check in game. Vendor prices for crafted armor were cut, quests from level 48 also pay gold, and the level 40 mount costs **100g** (90g at Honored). There's no WoW Token, and Blizzard is acting harder against gold buyers.

### Cited Findings
- **Hotfix Nov 10:** "Adjusted the minimum deposit amount to 1 copper (was 1 silver)" (with realm restarts). Vendors pay less for some crafted BS/LW/Tailoring armor (BoP items meant to be crafted once). Iron Grenade vendor price 5s → 1s 25c. Minor Arcane Elixir 25c → 5c. Journeyman Campfire Kit needs Cooking 90 (was 140). Coarse Grinding Stone gives skill-ups up to 90. Swiftstrike Shot/Swiftfeather Arrow no longer BoP. Fishing Rack lure fix. **"Most quests level 48 and higher now additionally reward gold."** Racial vendor mounts are now BoP — [Warcraft Tavern, Nov 10/11](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/class-economy-changes-wow-forever-hotfixes-for-november-10/)
- **Nov 12, Kaivax:** "We've temporarily removed Auction House changes from yesterday's update". They'll go live with the weekly maintenance on Nov 17. A player report on Nov 14 still showed 1s. Space4Games says EU is "on November 18" (no official EU time) — [kami-labs, Nov 16](https://kami-labs.fr/en/wow-forever/hotel-des-ventes-wow-forever-depot-1-cuivre/). Blizzard has published **neither a deposit formula nor an AH cut** for Forever — [same](https://kami-labs.fr/en/wow-forever/hotel-des-ventes-wow-forever-depot-1-cuivre/)
- **Live status (Nov 17/18):** one source (checked Nov 19) reports players still seeing 1s on Nov 11, 14 and early Nov 17. On Nov 18 one player said it was "officially in", and two players report the switch to 1c. **Not confirmed by Blizzard** — [search result summary of wow-forever.news / epiccarry / teamoverpowered](https://wow-forever.news/articles/wow-forever-auction-house-minimum-deposit-delay-november-2026-6b3396c97747); [epiccarry "Deposit Is Now 1 Copper"](https://epiccarry.com/blogs/wow-forever-auction-house-deposit/). The Nov 17 maintenance also restored UBRS difficulty and fixed the mail delay (2 min → 60 min as intended) — [same search](https://bindonforever.com/articles/wow-forever-auction-house-changes-november-17)
- **Correction to the local notes:** `GOLD-RECHERCHE-2026-11.md` says "since the Nov 10 hotfix, back to 1 copper". **That's wrong.** The change was pulled on Nov 12 and only came with the Nov 17/18 maintenance (per players).
- **Hotfix Nov 16:** the Fishing Rack gives lures to everyone nearby. Bloated Fish from the Fishing Rack effect have a higher drop chance and adjusted loot. Formula: Enchant Boots – Speed is now BoP. **No AH, Cooking, LW or Skinning changes** — [Warcraft Tavern, Nov 17](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/fishing-rack-adjustments-wow-forever-hotfixes-for-november-16/)
- Other dates (overview): Nov 8, dungeon kill XP +50%. Nov 13/14, no XP from trivial dungeon kills (beyond a 12-level gap). Item Restoration disabled for now — [search overview of Blizzard/Warcraft Tavern hotfix posts](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/more-fishing-tournament-winners-wow-forever-hotfixes-for-november-13/). A "Blackmaw Hold gold exploit" (Nov 16) shows up only at gamerfuzion and is **not in the official notes**, so it's unconfirmed — [gamerfuzion](https://www.gamerfuzion.com/world-of-warcraft-forever-november-16-hotfixes-vampiric-embrace-windfury-totem-blackmaw-hold-gold-exploit/)
- **Mount at 40:** Apprentice Riding + first mount = **100g** (90g at Honored with your home faction). Extra mount color 20g / 18g / 16g with Bartering (Bartering no longer discounts training since the Oct 15 build). Paladins and Warlocks get it free via class quest — [conquestcapped, Nov 16](https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-gold-making/). Another source says "50 gold at any class trainer", which contradicts this and is unconfirmed — [boosting.pro](https://boosting.pro/wow-forever-gold-guide/). Journeyman Riding: 1,000g (local note from Nov 8, beta prices).

- **Gold buying:** no WoW Token in Forever; Blizzard closes accounts that buy gold — [games.gg / search overview](https://games.gg/world-of-warcraft/guides/how-to-make-gold-in-wow-forever-gathering-the-auction-house-and-bans/). Blizzard is taking a tougher line on RMT in Forever ([Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/blizzard-plans-a-more-aggressive-stance-on-gold-buying-in-wow-forever/)). ### Inferences
- If the 1c minimum is live, **listing cheap goods (raw fish at 8–38c, Light Leather at 50c) is viable again**. Before that, a 1s deposit cost more than the item. Until you've confirmed it in game, an add-on should **read the deposit from the AH instead of hard-coding it**.
- The lower vendor prices for crafted armor remove the vendor floor for LW crafts. Profit = AH only.
- Main demand driver for levels 36–40 is **saving 100g for the mount**. Players spend little on goods; buyers are the 20–35 bracket, and from Dec 9 the level 60 raid preparation (Rugged/Thorium already rising).

### Gaps
- No official Blizzard confirmation that the 1c deposit is live after Nov 17/18.
- No new information on "internal auction error" (Blizzard Nov 9: "leaving copper out seems to help"), the 15-minute full-scan limit or "item not found" after Nov 10. Nothing found on fixes.
- No reliable data on server population, number of realms or Horde/Alliance balance in EU. "Faction balance" appears only in a Space4Games headline from Nov 13 (not fetched).
- No inflation index. Only single prices are available (falling raw materials at launch, now partly recovering).

## 4. What sells fast in the leveling bracket, and what is oversaturated?

### Takeaway
**Selling:** Thick armor kits (but crowded), Cured Rugged Hide, Salt Shaker output, Dusky Leather Armor (US), Camp Tent/Campfire Kit (small), Goldthorn Tea, niche fish crafts, and level-50+ materials (Rugged Leather, Thorium). **Oversaturated or losing money:** Pristine Leather/Hide (thousands listed), most leveling food, Iridescent Pearls, Frostsaber Leather (−90%), Medium/Heavy kits (skill-up only), and LW Commerce Authority gear (BoP).

### Cited Findings
- Thick kits Forceful/Mystic: 150+ listed per kit, margin 18–30s (Nov 11) — [timesaver LW](https://timesaver.gg/blog/wow-forever-leatherworking-recipes-gold). Thick Armor Kit US +23% in 7 days to 18s 99c (Nov 19) — [timesaver auction prices](https://timesaver.gg/wow-forever/auction-prices)
- Pristine Leather and Hide: over 3,000 listed each at 17s / 5s (Nov 11) — [timesaver LW](https://timesaver.gg/blog/wow-forever-leatherworking-recipes-gold); Pristine Leather ~5s (Nov 13) — [conquestcapped](https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-gold-making/)
- Cooking listing counts (US Alliance, Nov 19): Basic Campfire Kit ~6,967, Roasted Boar Meat ~4,840, Goblin Deviled Clams ~3,870, Boiled Clams ~2,950, Goldthorn Tea ~2,045, Crab Cake ~1,773, Dry Pork Ribs ~1,732. Spinefin Halibut only 35. The page runs the numbers together; I split them on a best-effort basis — [timesaver Cooking](https://timesaver.gg/tools/wow-forever/professions/cooking)
- Armor kits and the Camp Tent sell steadily while players level — [conquestcapped](https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-gold-making/). The biggest batch of new LW recipes is gear you wear, not gear you sell — [timesaver LW](https://timesaver.gg/blog/wow-forever-leatherworking-recipes-gold)
- Don't sell belts, tanned hides or intermediates you will need later before you've crafted the later recipes that use them (LW guide, German) — [conquestcapped LW (DE)](https://conquestcapped.com/guides/de/wow-forever/wow-forever-leatherworking/)
- From the local Nov 10 notes (still valid): bags, armor kits (Forceful/Mystic), quivers and reagent bags are leveling demand. Hotfix Nov 5: Zephras Isle vendors sell apprentice profession bags, so there is less demand for those.

### Inferences
- **For Tiry (Horde, level ~36, Cooking + LW):** the most promising options are Dusky Leather Armor, Thick kits at an even silver price without undercutting, Cured Thick/Heavy Hide only if they sell faster than the leather (Cured Thick 11.5s against Thick Leather ~7–8s), and Goldthorn Tea or niche fish. Avoid classic leveling food (wolf, boar, clams) because it sells below material cost.
- Sell raw fish only if the 1c deposit is confirmed. Otherwise use it for your own skill-ups or Goldthorn Tea.

### Gaps
- No sales rates or sell-through data for any item. Listing counts don't show turnover.
- No data on Barbaric, Guardian, Nightscape or Shadowskin items, Turtle Scale, or Medium Armor Kit in Forever.
- No EU Horde-specific data on what sells fast.
