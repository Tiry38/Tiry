# Research: Gold making in WoW: Forever (as of 2026-11-08)

Notes on sources: most Forever gold guides come from gold-selling or boost sites (timesaver.gg, lfcarry, conquestcapped, mmonster and others) and use beta-era data. They are marked [commercial]. I found **no Reddit threads** (r/woweconomy, r/classicwow) on Forever in the search index, and **no official Blizzard profession or AH announcement** was returned. Everything marked "unverified" has not been checked against a primary source.

**Brakes in launch week:**
- A flat **1s deposit** per listing. Forum complaints from 2026-11-05/06; no blue response yet. This makes listing cheap goods unprofitable. https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/what-is-going-on-with-the-auction-house/2402031
- Vendor prices for crafted weapons, armor and bars were cut by about 20–75% (beta build Oct 15), e.g. Azerothium Bar 50s→30s. Crafted wands were cut to 1s10c/2s. Mild Spices sell for 1c. Vendor flips with crafted goods are therefore weak. https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-gold-making/ , https://timesaver.gg/blog/wow-forever-gold-farming-guide
- Skinning: a creature can only be skinned "once again" (beta Oct 1). Mage AoE was nerfed. The SM chest loop is dead (conquestcapped).
- Spending targets: Apprentice Riding 100g at level 40 (90g at Honored), Journeyman 1,000g. Training 1–60 costs about 206g (conquestcapped / timesaver, beta prices).

**Systems:**
- **Waylaid Crates:** 8 crate types (Herbs, Ore, Textiles, Curiosities [white]; Fabrics, Ingots, Parts [green]) × 4 tiers (Apprentice/Journeyman from level 10, Expert from 20, Artisan from 35). Turn in at Marcy Baker (Three Corners, Redridge) or Dokimi (Crossroads). Rewards: white Apprentice 5 Favor + 2s50c, green Apprentice 10 Favor + 5s, white Journeyman 10 Favor + 5s, green Journeyman 20 Favor + 10s. Full material table: https://www.stillsixty.com/guides/waylaid-crates . **This creates steady material demand in fixed bundle sizes** (e.g. 20 Copper Bar, 10 Bronze Bar, 40 Linen, 20 Strange Dust).
- **Merchant's Favor recipes:** 30/45 Favor need no reputation, 60/120 need Friendly, 90/180+ need Honored (https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/guides/blacksmithing-profession-guide ; secondary source).
- **600+ (community count 884–968) new recipes**, e.g. 197 Blacksmithing plans and camp recipes (Sharpening Wheel / Anvil / Master Forge) (https://wowforeverguides.com/professions , https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/guides/blacksmithing-profession-guide ; unverified).
- **Black Market** at Powderfuse Port (Riverglades, about 78.1/51.9). Mister Graphed sells Black Lotus and Arcane Crystal for 50g each and Pyrite for 5g, with no limit. **That is a hard price ceiling** for those items (https://www.raidmuster.com/guides/wow-forever-black-market-vendor , https://lfcarry.com/guides/wow-forever-gold-economy ; beta info).
- **AH:** separate auction houses per ruleset (PvE/PvP/HC/RP) and faction. Neutral AH cut 15%, faction AH 5% (lfcarry, beta). Commodity-style purchase for stackable goods (the cheapest offers are picked automatically), so minimal undercutting is pointless (https://www.u4n.com/news/wow-forever-auction-house-guide-commodities-auctionator-and-why-undercutting-has-changed.html). The AH has a column for equip level and suffix stats (Oct 15).
- **Gift of Galloping** 150g (+10% mount speed for 15 days) – a gold sink (lfcarry).
- No WoW Token, no boost, GDKP banned (conquestcapped). Temporary economy holds on suspected gold buyers since 2026-10-13 (https://timesaver.gg/blog/wow-forever-is-it-safe-to-buy-gold).
- **Hotfix 2026-11-06:** Tanning Rack requires Leatherworking 125 (was 140). Ridgeshade Lurkers (Durotar) now have loot. Krek the Noxious loot was fixed (https://www.mmo-champion.com/content/14680-WoW-Forever-Hotfixes-November-6-2026).
